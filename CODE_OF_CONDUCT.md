# Code of Conduct

Hermas contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, safe, and constructive.

We will:

- Treat people with respect regardless of identity, background, experience,
  or point of view.
- Critique ideas and evidence without personal attacks.
- Respect boundaries and stop unwanted attention.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and
  publishing another person's private information.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently exclude participants whose conduct harms the community. Report
conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. Reports will be handled
confidentially, promptly, and without retaliation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
